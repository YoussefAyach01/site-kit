# site-kit: business one-pager

## Buyer and problem

Owners of small Moroccan businesses (restaurants, barbers, dentists, gyms) whose only web presence is a Facebook or Instagram page or a Google Maps pin. Customers ask "prix ?" and "horaires ?" in DMs all day. They want a real site that shows prices, hours and a WhatsApp button, in French and Arabic, without a monthly subscription.

## Offer and pricing

| Package                                                                             | MAD            | USD (approx.) |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------- |
| Setup: template customised with the client's content, FR + AR, delivered and online | 1,500 - 2,500  | 150 - 250     |
| Yearly care (domain renewal, hosting, 2 content edits)                              | 500 / year     | 50 / year     |
| Extras: client photos, Google Business profile setup                                | 300 - 500 each | 30 - 50       |

Cost to you: domain about 100 MAD/year; hosting free (GitHub or Cloudflare Pages). Margin is almost all your time, about 2 hours per client once the routine is known.

## First 5 customers

1. Walk the streets near you (or your university) and screenshot businesses with no site. Return in 2 days with their own name already in the demo template and show it on your phone. A live preview of "their" site sells better than a pitch.
2. Message businesses that have an Instagram page but no link in bio, via DM, offering one free page in exchange for a testimonial and a Google review. Convert the first 2 to paid extras afterwards.
3. Ask a dentist or gym owner you know for one introduction each: these sectors know each other and pay the most. Offer friends-and-family price for the first three to get case studies.

## Accounts needed to go live

None for the product itself. For each client: a domain (Namecheap or a .ma registrar such as through ANRT-accredited resellers), and free static hosting (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages). For your own demo: one hosting account.

## Honest limits

- Static sites: no online booking, ordering or payments (see qr-menu and booking-page for those).
- Arabic text was drafted by an AI assistant and must be proofread by a native speaker per client.
- Sample phone numbers, names and reviews are fictional placeholders and must all be replaced.
- Competing with Wix, Canva and cousins who "do sites for 500 MAD": you win on speed, bilingual/RTL quality and no monthly fee, not on price.
- Not tested in real browsers other than one headless Chrome check at 375px; Safari/iOS and Arabic font fallback on older Android were not checked.
